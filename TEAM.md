## Ownership

**The code in this repository belongs jointly to the collaborators of this repository**, the
members listed above, in equal parts — not to the account that hosts it. The GitHub owner holds
the repository settings on the team's behalf; that role confers no greater ownership of the code.

All rights are reserved by the collaborators. The code is public to read, but nobody outside the
team may copy, modify or redistribute it.

The hackathon rules leave ownership with the team (§7: *"All Submissions remain the intellectual
property of the individuals or organizations that developed them"*). Submitting only lets the
organisers show the project for judging and promotion, for 12 months.

## Representative

**Representative:** _(name)_

The rules require one person to register the team, enter the submission and act on the team's
behalf (§3, "Team Representation"). Cash prizes are paid to that person alone, and the
organisers explicitly take no responsibility for what happens next:

> It is the responsibility of the winning Entrant's Representative to allocate the Prize among
> their Team's or Organization's participating members as the Representative deems appropriate.
> Neither the Sponsor nor the Administrator has any responsibility for how a Prize is shared
> among Team or Organization members. (§8, "Prize delivery")

## Prize split

Any cash prize is shared **equally, 25% each**, after the bank and currency fees the rules put on
the recipient. Serverpod Cloud credits are non-transferable and go to the account the
Representative registers; how the team uses them is decided together.

Each member is responsible for the taxes on their own share in their own country (§8, "Fees and
taxes").
